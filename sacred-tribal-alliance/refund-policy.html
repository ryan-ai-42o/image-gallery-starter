Refund Policy

All arrangements at Sacred Tribal Alliance are handled through direct conversation, not a shopping cart. This page explains how changes, cancellations, and disputes are handled.

Last updated

October 2026

No purchases on this site

This site does not currently process payments or complete transactions. There is nothing to purchase here directly. All arrangements are initiated by inquiry and conducted through direct correspondence with Sacred Tribal Alliance.

Because no purchases can be made on this site, no refund request can originate from it at this time. This policy describes the standards that will govern transactions when a checkout system is active, and the process for resolving any issue with a prior arrangement.

Inquiry-based arrangements

Every arrangement begins with a conversation about sourcing, preparation, safety, and fit. Price, quantity, and logistics are agreed upon directly before any payment is requested. No obligation is created by submitting an inquiry — an arrangement is final only when both parties have confirmed the terms in writing.

Cancellation before shipment

If an arrangement has been confirmed and payment collected but the order has not yet shipped, contact us at sacredtribalalliance@gmail.com as soon as possible. We will work to cancel the order and issue a full refund if the material has not been prepared or shipped. Cancellation requests after shipment is initiated may not be honored.

Returns

Due to the nature of the materials — traditional plant preparations that cannot be restocked once opened — we do not accept returns of opened or used items. If you receive a shipment that is materially different from what was agreed upon in your arrangement, or if there is a shipping or fulfillment error, contact us within 7 days of receipt and we will work to resolve the issue.

Damaged or incorrect shipments

If your order arrives damaged or contains the wrong material, email sacredtribalalliance@gmail.com within 7 days of delivery with a description of the issue. We will arrange a replacement or refund as appropriate.

Refund method and timing

When a refund is approved, it will be issued to the original payment method used. Processing time depends on the payment processor and your financial institution, typically 5–10 business days. We will notify you when the refund has been initiated.

Disputes

We prefer to resolve any issue through direct communication before a dispute is filed with a payment processor. If you have a concern, email us first at sacredtribalalliance@gmail.com. We will respond within 2 business days.

Contact

Refund or order questions: sacredtribalalliance@gmail.com
