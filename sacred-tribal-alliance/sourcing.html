Sourcing

Where the medicine comes from

Sourcing isn't a detail to be summarized on a product page. It is the relationship. Every inquiry includes a real conversation about origin, preparation, and the people involved.

Our principles

What we know, we say. What we don't know, we say that too.

We do not source from wholesale catalogs or anonymous intermediaries. Every batch comes with provenance we can actually speak to: the region, the knowledge-holder or community involved in preparation, and as much of the chain as we have visibility into.

We do not claim to "directly support tribes" as a marketing statement. When a percentage of a transaction flows back toward source communities, we describe the specific mechanism — not as a tagline.

We hold sourcing as an ongoing relationship, not a one-time vetting check. When something changes about a source, we update what we're able to offer rather than continuing with compromised supply.

Preparation

Prepared by knowledge-holders, not repackaged

Hapé and Sananga are prepared through specific processes that vary by tradition and blend. The quality of what arrives depends on the integrity of that preparation — the materials, the ratio, the method, and the intention brought to it.

We do not reprocess or re-blend what we receive. We work only with preparations from knowledge-holders who prepared them, and we do not claim preparation knowledge we don't have.
