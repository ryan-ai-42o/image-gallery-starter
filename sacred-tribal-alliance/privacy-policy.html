Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Sacred Tribal Alliance collects, how it is used, and how it is protected. This site does not currently process payments or store personal data on a server.

Last updated

October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the website operated by Sacred Tribal Alliance ("we," "us," or "our") at this domain. By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

Contact inquiries. This site's contact form does not submit data to a server. When you use the form, it opens your own email application with a prefilled message. The information in that message is transmitted through your email provider and delivered to sacredtribalalliance@gmail.com. We retain that correspondence in our email inbox in order to respond to your inquiry.

Age confirmation. When you confirm you are 21 or older, a session token is stored in your browser's sessionStorage. This token is local to your browser and session only. It is not transmitted to us or to any server. It expires when you close your browser tab.

No analytics or tracking. This site does not currently use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any third-party tracking or advertising scripts.

Fonts. This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Google's servers receive a request when the page loads, which may include your IP address and browser information. Google's privacy policy governs that data: policies.google.com/privacy.

How we use information

Information you send us through email is used solely to respond to your inquiry and to conduct the conversation about sourcing, preparation, and whether an arrangement is appropriate. We do not sell, rent, or share your contact information with third parties.

Payments and transactions

This site does not currently offer any purchasing functionality. No payment information is collected or processed on this site. When a transaction model is active, this policy will be updated to reflect the payment processor used and how transaction data is handled.

Data retention

Email correspondence is retained in our inbox as long as needed to manage the inquiry relationship. You may request that we delete your correspondence by emailing sacredtribalalliance@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us directly at sacredtribalalliance@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site's functionality changes — particularly when a payment or checkout system is added. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
