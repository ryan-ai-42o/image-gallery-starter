Our Story

The people behind Sacred Tribal Alliance

Brennan Ledet and Stephanie Bourque have traveled across multiple countries, working with Indigenous communities and learning about traditional medicines in their cultural settings. These experiences inform a shared approach centered on listening, respectful relationships, and care for the traditions surrounding these medicines.

Brennan Ledet

Brennan's work centers on traditional plant medicines, particularly Hapé and its ceremonial uses. His travels and time spent learning with Indigenous communities have shaped his understanding of the different preparations and traditions surrounding these medicines. He approaches this work as an ongoing learning process, with respect for the people who carry that knowledge and an emphasis on honest conversation about its origins and use.

Stephanie Bourque

Stephanie is a Louisiana-based Kambo practitioner whose work also includes Hapé and Sananga. She has traveled across multiple countries, working with Indigenous communities and learning about traditional practices in their cultural settings. Her approach emphasizes attentive care, preparation, and respect for the people and traditions from which these practices come.

Why this exists

Honest access over easy packaging

There are a lot of people offering plant medicines online with almost no context about source, preparation, or protocol. And there are people who want access to these materials but don't have the relationships or knowledge to vet what they're getting.

Sacred Tribal Alliance sits in the middle of that — not as a retailer, but as a bridge. Every inquiry starts with a conversation. Every offering comes with what we know about where it came from and how it was prepared. If we don't know something, we say so.

This is not a drop-shipping operation and it is not spiritual tourism. It is a slow, relationship-based way of making these traditions accessible without stripping them of what makes them worth accessing.
