Shilajit

A dense, dark mineral-resin that forms slowly in high-altitude rock formations over long periods of time. Distinct from our plant-medicine offerings — Shilajit comes from the Altai region and carries its own traditional context of use separate from Amazonian ceremony.

Shilajit is not a plant medicine. It forms from the slow compression of organic material between rock layers at high elevation — a geological process that produces a resin rich in minerals and fulvic acid. It has been part of traditional practice in Siberian, Central Asian, and Ayurvedic traditions for centuries, used as a general tonic and mineral source.

We source our Shilajit from the Altai region through VibeMedX. Like everything we offer, it comes with a conversation — how it was collected, what testing has been done, and how it fits into the person's current practice. We do not blend it with the Amazonian medicines or represent them as part of the same lineage.

It is offered as a separate section of our catalog — a mineral-resin, not a ceremonial plant preparation.
