Reciprocity

How access works

These medicines belong to living traditions. What we do here is a small bridge — not ownership, not a retail channel. The people who carry these traditions come first.

Access model

Inquiry first. Always.

There is no shopping cart. There is no one-click buy. Every arrangement starts with a conversation about who you are, what you're approaching this with, and whether what we have is an appropriate fit.

This is not gatekeeping for its own sake. It is the only responsible way to handle materials that carry this much context — sourcing, preparation, safety, protocol, and the relationship between the buyer and what they're receiving.

Price, quantity, and logistics are discussed during that conversation, not listed on a page. This is an open-item flagged for the full legal and checkout review — no transaction happens until that process is complete.

Flow-back

What returns to source

We do not use "gives back to tribes" as a marketing claim. When a portion of what flows in here can be traced back to the people and communities the medicine came from, we describe that mechanism specifically.

The current state of that relationship is part of every sourcing conversation. We do not claim more than we can account for, and we do not use community language to sell product.
