Hapé

Blend catalog

Each blend is a specific preparation tradition. Descriptions reflect what these blends are traditionally associated with — not medical claims or promises of outcomes.

Gentle / Heart-centered

Tsunu

One of the most widely known Hapé blends. Traditionally associated with openness, gentleness, and heart-centered ceremony.

Samsara

Traditionally associated with gentle clearing and a warm, heart-centered quality.

Flor de Laranjeira

Orange blossom blend. Traditionally associated with lightness, gentleness, and floral opening qualities in ceremony.

Cacao

Incorporates cacao, which carries its own ceremony tradition. Traditionally associated with heart-opening intention in ceremonial use.

Bobinsana

Bobinsana is a river plant with a long tradition in Amazonian plant medicine. Traditionally associated with emotional clearing and inner warmth in ceremony.

Rose Vermea

Red rose blend. Traditionally associated with heart-centered intention and gentle opening in ceremony.

Cumaru

Made with tonka bean (Dipteryx odorata). Traditionally associated with warmth, sweetness, and heart-centered ceremonial use.

Grounding / Forest-rooted

Paricá

A deeply grounding blend rooted in forest tradition. Traditionally associated with earthing, stability, and ceremonial clearing.

Murici

Murici is a fruit tree of the Brazilian cerrado and Amazon. Traditionally associated with grounding and forest-rooted intention.

Mulateiro

Made from Mulateiro bark, a traditional Amazonian tree medicine. Traditionally associated with forest-rooted clearing and vitality in ceremony.

Jurema

Jurema Preta carries a long tradition in northeastern Brazilian plant medicine. Traditionally associated with depth, clearing, and ceremonial use.

Pixuri

An aromatic Amazonian blend. Traditionally associated with forest-rooted grounding and clearing in ceremony.

Samaúma

The Samaúma (Ceiba) is one of the great trees of Amazonia. Traditionally associated with expansiveness, ceremony, and strong grounding.

Palo Santo

Made with holy wood from Bursera graveolens. Traditionally associated with clearing, protection, and purification in ceremony across Andean and Amazonian traditions.

Canela de Velho

"Old man's cinnamon." Traditionally associated with vitality, strength, and elder-energy in ceremonial use.

Stronger — inquiry and safety conversation required

Veia de Pajé

"Shaman's vein." A strong ceremonial blend. Traditionally associated with deep ceremonial work. Requires a full conversation before any offering is arranged.

Katsumari

A stronger blend from specific Amazonian traditions. Traditionally associated with ceremonial depth. Requires a safety conversation before any arrangement.

Angico

Made from Angico bark. A strong traditional preparation. Full inquiry and safety conversation required.

Chiric Sanango

A preparation associated with plant spirit work in Amazonian tradition. One of the stronger blends in the catalog. Requires detailed conversation before any offering.

Canaleiro

Traditionally associated with strong clearing. Full inquiry required.

7 Hiervas

Seven herbs blend. A ceremonial preparation. Full inquiry and safety conversation required before any arrangement.

Gesileo

A ceremony-oriented blend. Requires detailed conversation before any offering is arranged.

Mentholado

Cooling and clearing blend. Traditionally associated with breath, cooling sensation, and clearing in ceremony.

All blends are available by inquiry only. Descriptions reflect traditional associations — they are not medical claims and do not describe effects that will occur for any individual. Hapé contains concentrated mapacho tobacco and is not appropriate for everyone.

Blends in the "Stronger" category require a detailed safety conversation before any offering is arranged. Reach out via the contact page to begin.
