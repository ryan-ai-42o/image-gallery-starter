Medicines

Sananga

An eye medicine made from the roots and bark of Tabernaemontana undulata and related species. Traditionally used by Amazonian peoples in ceremony for purposes of spiritual and perceptual clarity.

Sananga is prepared from specific plant roots and bark and applied directly to the eyes. The experience is intense — a strong burning or stinging sensation that typically subsides within several minutes. Within these traditions, this is understood as part of the medicine's effect, not a side effect to be avoided.

It is used in ceremony contexts and is not a casual or recreational preparation. All inquiries include a conversation about preparation, safety, and fit before any arrangement is made.

Before you inquire

Sananga causes a strong, temporary burning or stinging sensation and may blur vision for several minutes after application. Do not drive or operate machinery immediately after use. Do not share applicators between people. This is not an eye treatment for any medical condition. Legal status varies by jurisdiction — it is your responsibility to know the laws where you are.
